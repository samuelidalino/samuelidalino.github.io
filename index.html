How I would approach a Zendesk SDR account

Sample account workflow

  1. Research the customer-support environment and growth context.
  2. Identify the likely CX / Support leadership persona.
  3. Build a pain hypothesis: fragmented channels, slow response times, scaling service volume or limited visibility.
  4. Start with a concise consultative message rather than a generic product pitch.
  5. Use discovery questions to validate pain, urgency and current workflow.
  6. Document context cleanly in CRM and hand off a qualified opportunity to the AE.

Sample first touch

Hi {First Name}, I noticed your support operation is scaling across multiple customer channels. I’m curious how your team is currently managing visibility, response times and handoffs as volume grows. Would it be useful to compare how other teams are simplifying that workflow?
